What these findings mean

When a request depends on an attribute, missing or unusable product evidence prevents verification of that attribute. The share of traffic affected is unmeasured: listing frequency is not request frequency. This audit measures published evidence, not product truth, request interpretation accuracy, or financial coverage.

Suggested minimum: size. Most-published observed selection axis; otherwise most-published structured product attribute. Suggestion only; reviewer must select the floor. Review separate category cohorts before treating this minimum as necessary or sufficient. If no minimum is found, every listing remains below it; an empty catalogue has no rate.

Publication by attribute

Usable publication includes consistent typed readings from structured fields or labelled prose. Minimum-eligible evidence additionally requires an exact merchant source and excludes advice. Missing means no nonempty source; present but unusable means a source exists but cannot support a consistent typed reading.
